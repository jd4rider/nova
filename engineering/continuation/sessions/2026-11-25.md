## Continuation — Parser AST arena (bootstrap regression)

Generation 2 of the self-build was killed by the OS (signal 9) after ~107s and
5.68 GB peak RSS; `sample` showed a 68.7 GB physical footprint and mapped the
stack to `a_item`/`a_fn`/`a_block`/`a_stmt`/`a_expr` and `a_add`, not codegen.
The cause was value semantics: `a_add` copied both growing AST lists for every
node, so parsing was quadratic. A synthetic 160-function source already
allocated 570 MB.

Nodes and links now append to a C-side arena (`nova_ast_*` in
`runtime/runtime.c`) by index; `a_parse` materialises the two lists once at the
end into fresh local lists, which `nova_list_push` grows in place. `a_primary`
reads the in-progress node fields through arena accessors. Only `Int`/`String`
cross the extern boundary, which the Rust seed's sema requires.

(previously >107s / 5.68 GB and SIGKILL); generations 1–3 build in
2.15s/3.08 GB, 2.16s/2.91 GB and 2.29s/2.90 GB; all three emit byte-identical
IR (`sha256 7e2368452e2535796a3213c92ba4d7e1346b86e1`) while linked executables
